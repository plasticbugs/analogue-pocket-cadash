**Analogizer support.** Cadash now works with RndMnkIII's
[Analogizer](https://github.com/RndMnkIII/Analogizer) adapter: the board's
own picture on a CRT, and native controllers through SNAC. Confirmed on a
Pocket with an Analogizer and a CRT.

### What's new

- **Video out of the adapter's VGA port**: RGBS, RGsB, YPbPr, Y/C (NTSC or
  PAL), or scandoubled for a VGA monitor. The signal is the board's own:
  320×240 at 15.73 kHz, 60.03 Hz; a normal 15 kHz set will do.
- **Set up from the core's own menu**, no `analogizer.bin` file needed:
  **Analogizer** (Off, On, or "On, Pocket off" for the CRT only),
  **Analogizer Video**, **SNAC Adapter**, **SNAC Assignment**. Off by
  default; with it off the core plays as before.
- **H / V Position** centre the picture on the CRT (28 pixels left to 20
  right, 12 lines up to 4 down).
- **SNAC controllers** (DB15, NES, SNES, PC Engine, PlayStation) for players
  1 and 2.
- The memory clock's timing moved to make room for the adapter; play was
  checked on a Pocket with it.

### Menu changes

To fit the Pocket's 16 menu entries, three left the menu and stay at their
defaults: **Scanlines** and **Shadow Mask** (off) and **Flip Screen** (the
board's flip DIP, normal).

### Before you install

**With this core the Pocket powers the cartridge slot.** Take any game
cartridge out first. Set the adapter up as its
[How to use it](https://github.com/RndMnkIII/Analogizer/wiki/How-to-use-it%3F)
page says (SNAC switch on A, 5 V into its USB-C port, the audio cable into
the Pocket's headphone socket).

### Installing

Unzip onto the Pocket SD card root, overwriting previous files. ROMs are not
included: build the image from your own MAME romset with the included
`mra_build.py` and put it in `Assets/cadash/common/`.
